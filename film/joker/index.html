The Kernel take

A middling 59 Kernel Score ("Mixed") didn't stop Joker from returning roughly 6× its combined budget and marketing spend — critical consensus and commercial outcome are different signals.

Director track record: Todd Phillips

Joker's 59 is right around Todd Phillips' 57-point career average — not a critical outlier for him, just a much bigger, more expensive version of the reception his films usually get.

Lead track record: Joaquin Phoenix

Joker (59) sits well below Joaquin Phoenix's average across these roles (74) — one of his more divisive reviews as an actor, even though it wasn't a low point for Phillips as director. Metacritic's own site states a lower all-time career score (62) reflecting titles outside this sample.

Box office

A 3.5× domestic multiplier and just a -41.9% second-weekend drop are unusually strong holds for a wide release — better than most blockbusters, despite critics calling it only "Mixed." Real audiences kept showing up after the reviews were already in.
