The Kernel take

The Death of Robin Hood was not a limited or day-and-date streaming release masking a real audience -- it opened wide in roughly 1,762-1,782 domestic theaters on June 19, 2026, before collapsing 78.5% in its second weekend and dropping to just 25 theaters by its fourth (Box Office Mojo), a hold pattern typical of a wide release that failed to connect commercially. That commercial collapse sits alongside a genuinely mixed-to-positive critical reception (Metacritic score 60, "Mixed or Average", 51% positive across 35 reviews), so the box office weakness looks like an audience/marketing problem for an unconventional, bleak R-rated take on the legend rather than a reviews problem or a deliberately small release.
