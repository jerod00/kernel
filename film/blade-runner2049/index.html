The Kernel take

An 81 Kernel Score ("Universal Acclaim") with 87% positive reviews and zero negative reviews reflects near-unanimous critical praise for Blade Runner 2049. Commercially, though, it returned only about 1.7× its reported $150M budget (marketing spend undisclosed) — a notable box-office disappointment for a big-budget studio release, despite the reviews.

Director track record: Denis Villeneuve

Blade Runner 2049's 81 sits above Denis Villeneuve's 75-point career average across these films, tied with Arrival as his second-highest score behind only Sicario (82).

Lead track record: Ryan Gosling

Blade Runner 2049 (81) is well above Ryan Gosling's 66-point average across these roles — one of his better-reviewed films, though still shy of La La Land (94), his high point in this sample.
