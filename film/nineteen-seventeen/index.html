The Kernel take

A 78 Kernel Score ("Generally Favorable") reflects strong reviews for 1917, with 86% positive and only 2% negative. Commercially it returned about 2.1× its combined $100M budget and $115M marketing spend — right around the industry breakeven threshold, a solid but not spectacular outcome for a $215M all-in war epic.

Director track record: Sam Mendes

1917's 78 is well above Sam Mendes' 67-point average across these films — one of his best-reviewed works, behind only American Beauty (84) and Skyfall (81).

Lead track record: George MacKay

1917 (78) is well above George MacKay's 62-point average across these roles — clearly his highest-profile and best-reviewed lead performance in this sample.

Box office

Budget $100M · Marketing $115M · Box office $446.1M

1917 actually grew +6.9% in its second week rather than dropping, reflecting its slow-building platform release strategy (a limited Christmas Day bow before wide expansion in week three, where weekly gross jumped to $37.0M). The resulting 274× domestic-to-opening-week multiplier is a product of that tiny limited-release denominator, not a signal of unusually strong legs the way that number would read for a wide-release blockbuster.
